The Land Yacht

Ingramport, Nova Scotia St. Margaret's Bay Campaign complete · Two films delivered
Overview — 001

The challenge

The Land Yacht is a one-of-a-kind stay on St. Margaret's Bay — and it is more than a rental. Beneath the water at the property's edge, its owners are placing living-reef structures to grow new marine habitat along the shoreline.

A listings page can't carry that. The property needed both the place and the stewardship behind it to be visible — and felt — before a guest ever arrives.

The approach

We filmed the property and the Living Reef initiative as two connected pieces: one introducing the stay itself, and one documenting the reef work taking shape in the bay.

Everything was shot on location and delivered in formats made for the web and for the property's social channels — landscape films for the site, vertical edits for social.
